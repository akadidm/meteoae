    The warmest month in Al Faqa is July, with average highs of 45 °C and lows of 31 °C. The coolest month is January, with average highs of 25 °C and lows of 13 °C.

    Rain is rare: about 81 mm in a year, most of it in January.

    The most pleasant time of year, with highs between 20 °C and 30 °C and little rain, is usually from December to February.
