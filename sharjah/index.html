    Climate in Sharjah

    In October, Sharjah usually has highs around 36 °C and lows around 28 °C, with about 2.6 mm of rain.

    The warmest month in Sharjah is July, with average highs of 41 °C and lows of 32 °C. The coolest month is January, with average highs of 24 °C and lows of 18 °C.

    Sharjah gets about 108 mm of rain a year; the wettest month is January (28 mm). Almost no rain falls from May to October.

    The most pleasant time of year, with highs between 20 °C and 30 °C and little rain, is usually from December to March.
