    The warmest month in Al Rams is June, with average highs of 39 °C and lows of 31 °C. The coolest month is January, with average highs of 23 °C and lows of 20 °C.

    Al Rams gets about 132 mm of rain a year; the wettest month is January (39 mm). Almost no rain falls from May to October.

    The most pleasant time of year, with highs between 20 °C and 30 °C and little rain, is usually from November to March.
