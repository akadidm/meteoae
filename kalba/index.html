    The warmest month in Kalba is June, with average highs of 43 °C and lows of 31 °C. The coolest month is January, with average highs of 26 °C and lows of 17 °C.

    Kalba gets about 148 mm of rain a year; the wettest month is January (29 mm). Almost no rain falls in May, September, and October.

    The most pleasant time of year, with highs between 20 °C and 30 °C and little rain, is usually from December to February.
