    Climate in Al Bidiyah

    In October, Al Bidiyah usually has highs around 36 °C and lows around 27 °C, with about 3.8 mm of rain.

    The warmest month in Al Bidiyah is June, with average highs of 41 °C and lows of 31 °C. The coolest month is January, with average highs of 25 °C and lows of 18 °C.

    Al Bidiyah gets about 122 mm of rain a year; the wettest month is January (32 mm). Almost no rain falls from May to October.

    The most pleasant time of year, with highs between 20 °C and 30 °C and little rain, is usually from December to February.
