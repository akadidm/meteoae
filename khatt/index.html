    The warmest month in Khatt is June, with average highs of 41 °C and lows of 31 °C. The coolest month is January, with average highs of 25 °C and lows of 18 °C.

    Khatt gets about 122 mm of rain a year; the wettest month is January (32 mm). Almost no rain falls from May to October.

    The most pleasant time of year, with highs between 20 °C and 30 °C and little rain, is usually from December to March.
