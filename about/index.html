    About WeatherPlus

    WeatherPlus publishes the weather today and the forecast for the days ahead, hour by hour, for every city it covers.

    Forecasts are model output and are updated several times a day. We do not fill gaps with invented values: when a measure is missing, the page says so. See data and methodology for the details.

    Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo under the CC BY 4.0 licence, from the numerical weather models of national weather services. Place names, coordinates and population figures come from GeoNames.

    Climate averages come from the NASA POWER project (NASA Langley Research Center).
