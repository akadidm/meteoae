    The warmest month in Al Dhaid is July, with average highs of 40 °C and lows of 31 °C. The coolest month is January, with average highs of 23 °C and lows of 17 °C.

    Al Dhaid gets about 108 mm of rain a year; the wettest month is January (28 mm). Almost no rain falls from May to October.

    The most pleasant time of year, with highs between 20 °C and 30 °C and little rain, is usually from November to March.
