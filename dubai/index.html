    Climate in Dubai

    In October, Dubai usually has highs around 35 °C and lows around 28 °C, with about 1.6 mm of rain.

    The warmest month in Dubai is August, with average highs of 40 °C and lows of 32 °C. The coolest month is January, with average highs of 23 °C and lows of 18 °C.

    Dubai gets about 102 mm of rain a year; the wettest month is January (23 mm). Almost no rain falls in May, June, July, September, and October.

    The most pleasant time of year, with highs between 20 °C and 30 °C and little rain, is usually from November to March.
